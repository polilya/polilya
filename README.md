### Hi, I'm Ilia Poludkin 👋
<sub>Earlier papers are published as Ilya E. Poludkin.</sub>

Biomedical engineer in Eindhoven. I design, build and validate medical devices — mechanics, electronics, simulation and experiments — and use Python and ML to make sense of the data they produce. MSc student in Soft Tissue Engineering and Mechanobiology at TU/e (2027); MSc with Honours in Biophotonics and Tissue Engineering, Bauman MSTU.

[LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/polilya) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=x5zcWz0AAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3198-3612)

#### Devices
- **Robotic catheter platform** for endovascular surgery — mechanics, PCB control panel, C/Arduino firmware, force feedback calibrated against an Instron reference. Patent RU223353U1; IEEE EDM 2024 prize.
- **Ultrasound thrombolysis device**, built from scratch: driver electronics, emitter assembly, COMSOL field and temperature models, layered tissue phantoms, preclinical animal trials.
- **Brain-on-chip** — four-channel PDMS microfluidic neurovascular unit model (MSc thesis); patent application filed.
